About Us

HairByJay started with a simple frustration: too many wigs promised luxury and delivered thin lace, dull strands, and colours that faded in a month. Jay set out to build a small, carefully curated collection instead — the kind of hair she'd want to wear herself.

Every piece we sell is chosen by hand for its density, its shine, and how naturally the lace sits against the skin. We keep the range small on purpose, so every unit meets the same standard.

We're based in Sandton and ship across the country, but we still treat every order like a client walking into a studio — with advice on colour, cut, and care included.

Quality first

100% human hair, double-wefted, with lace fronts that are pre-plucked and bleached for a seamless hairline.

Honest pricing

No inflated "sale" prices. What you see on the products page is what you pay, every day.

Personal service

Not sure which texture or colour suits you? Send an enquiry and Jay will help you choose.
